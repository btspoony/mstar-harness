- **A head-only waiver is never a merge authorization.** The §5.1b empty-increment exit and the pre-merge head reconciliation now state the §5.3 rule: a marker-less bot with no inheritance path either settles on its own new head or clears a blocking finding or a sub-threshold score with a written waiver naming that finding or score; a waiver naming only a head is an escalation record only and authorizes nothing.

- **只点名 head 的 waive 永不构成 merge 授权。** §5.1b 空增量出口与 merge 前 head 对账现统一为 §5.3 的规则：没有继承路径的无 marker bot 只能在自己的新 head 上结算，或就某个 blocking finding / 未达门的分数取得点名该 finding 或分数的书面 waive；只点名 head 的 waive 仅是升级记录，不构成任何授权。
