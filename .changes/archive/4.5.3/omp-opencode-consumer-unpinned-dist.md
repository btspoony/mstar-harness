- **The omp and OpenCode consumers stop pinning their build output, so `pack-consumers` can verify a fresh checkout.** `packages/omp/dist` and `packages/opencode/dist` bytes depend on the build environment, so a manifest written on one machine reported `consumer.digest-mismatch` for `packages/omp/dist/hooks/pre/mstar-gates.js` on the next. Both keep their source closure, runtime floor, capability and entrypoint declarations, and the copied instruction trees; their generated closure is empty and the entrypoint is proved by existence and non-emptiness, as the `cli`, `dsh` and `engine` consumers already did. `zcode` is now the only consumer that pins generated bytes, because its hook is committed rather than built.

- **omp 与 OpenCode consumer 不再钉死构建产出，`pack-consumers` 因此能在全新检出上完成校验。** `packages/omp/dist` 与 `packages/opencode/dist` 的字节依赖构建环境，一台机器写出的 manifest 在另一台上报 `consumer.digest-mismatch`（`packages/omp/dist/hooks/pre/mstar-gates.js`）。两者保留源码闭包、运行时下限、能力声明、entrypoint 声明与复制的指令树；generated 闭包为空，entrypoint 改用存在性与非空校验——与 `cli`、`dsh`、`engine` 的既有做法一致。`zcode` 成为唯一仍钉死 generated 字节的 consumer，因为它的 hook 是提交物而非构建产出。
