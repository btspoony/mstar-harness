- Added the **retained accepted-body inventory** to the recovery protocol (§7 R4). `backupStore` now freezes, under the same §4.3 maintenance exclusion that quiesces the copy, one inventory of the file-native bodies a SQLite copy does not carry — `notes.jsonl`, `agent-flow.jsonl`, the `agent-flow-ids.jsonl` identity index, `workflow-ledger-cursors.json`, `omp-launches.json`, every sealed `agent-flow-history/chunk-NNNNNN.jsonl` and the root-level `snapshots/engine-status.json` selection/cache body — recording each body's whole-byte SHA-256, its ordered accepted-record identities (the SHA-256 of every LF-terminated line, the LF excluded, exactly §5's index rule), its unterminated trailing record and whether it carries the durable selection facts. The inventory is written canonically beside the copy (`<image>.retained.json`), so a recovery point describes its bodies rather than only its database.
- **Backup and restore preflight refuse a present `agent-flow-compaction.json`** (§5): a tail/index/cursor/history set with an unfinished compaction cannot be captured or restored, and a partial restore that omitted that in-flight transaction would be corruption rather than an accepted duplicate-history residual. The refusal names the journal and writes nothing — no image, no inventory document, no body byte.
- **Versioned the receipt and the loss payload.** `BackupReceipt.receiptVersion` is now `BACKUP_RECEIPT_VERSION` (2) and carries `retained`; the recovery payload is `EXECUTION_RECOVERY_PROTOCOL_VERSION` (2) and its preview additionally carries `version`, `retainedDifferences`, `retainedDigest` and `retainedLiveDigest`. `restoreExecutionBackup` refuses a preview of another loss-payload generation **by name** instead of restoring without the body loss it cannot describe, and the migration gate keeps reading a version-1 receipt (which needs no bodies).
- `previewExecutionRestore` now compares the point's frozen inventory with the live bodies and lists the difference per body: `deleted` (the point froze it, the live store has not), `replaced` (the accepted records no longer match), `preserved` (an append-only extension §7 keeps rather than deletes) and `selection` (the body carrying the durable selection/cache facts). The digest covers all of it, a `deleted`/`replaced`/`selection` difference joins the exact-`acceptLossDigest` gate, and an interrupted append the point did not record — no accepted identity, so no hash reconciles it — refuses the whole inventory with both stores and every body retained and asks for the named **explicit salvage scope**.
- `restoreExecutionBackup` never rolls the bodies back: it re-freezes them by exact digest immediately before the replacement (a body that moved since the approval refuses), records `retainedDigest`/`retainedLiveDigest`/`retainedDifferences` on the durable receipt so a crash is decidable for the body set too, and returns them on the receipt. The epoch advance, the stale-refusal and the preservation of historical ids are unchanged; the retained bodies are explicitly described as not rolled back by `requiredRebind()`.
- One spelling of the §4.3 maintenance exclusion now lives in `store-activation.ts` (`withExecutionMaintenanceLock`) and is imported by the recovery module instead of duplicated, and `backupStoreUnderExclusion` is the same freeze for a caller that already holds it (the restore's own safety point).
- Tests: new `execution-populated.test.ts` (populated graph, staged/activated under real validated coverage, plus the recovery and refusal cycles), new `Phase 2b` cases in `execution-recovery.test.ts` and new `retained bodies` cases in `store-activation.test.ts`.

- 为恢复协议（§7 R4）新增**保留接受体清单**。`backupStore` 现在在冻结副本的同一 §4.3 maintenance exclusion 下，冻结 SQLite 副本并不携带的文件原生 body 清单——`notes.jsonl`、`agent-flow.jsonl`、`agent-flow-ids.jsonl` 身份索引、`workflow-ledger-cursors.json`、`omp-launches.json`、每个封存 `agent-flow-history/chunk-NNNNNN.jsonl` 以及根级 `snapshots/engine-status.json` 选择/缓存 body——逐条记录整体字节的 SHA-256、按序接受记录身份（每条以 LF 结尾行去掉 LF 的 SHA-256，即 §5 的索引规则）、未终结尾记录，以及是否承载持久选择事实。清单以规范形式写在副本旁（`<image>.retained.json`），使恢复点不只描述数据库，也描述其 body。
- **备份与恢复 preflight 拒绝存在 `agent-flow-compaction.json`**（§5）：存在未完成 compaction 的 tail/index/cursor/history 集合既不能捕获也不能恢复，部分恢复遗漏该在途事务是损坏，而不是可接受的重复历史残差。拒绝会点名该 journal 且不写入任何内容——无镜像、无清单文档、无 body 字节变化。
- **为收据与 loss payload 版本化。** `BackupReceipt.receiptVersion` 现为 `BACKUP_RECEIPT_VERSION`（2）并携带 `retained`；恢复 payload 为 `EXECUTION_RECOVERY_PROTOCOL_VERSION`（2），其 preview 另携带 `version`、`retainedDifferences`、`retainedDigest` 与 `retainedLiveDigest`。`restoreExecutionBackup` 对其它 loss-payload 世代**按名拒绝**，而不是在无法描述 body 损失的情况下照常恢复；migration 关卡仍可读取 version-1 收据（其不需要 body）。
- `previewExecutionRestore` 现将点的冻结清单与实时 body 比较，并按 body 列差：`deleted`（点冻结了、实时已无）、`replaced`（接受记录不再匹配）、`preserved`（§7 予以保留而非删除的追加扩展）与 `selection`（承载持久选择/缓存事实的 body）。摘要覆盖全部内容，`deleted`/`replaced`/`selection` 差进入精确 `acceptLossDigest` 闸门；点未记录的未终结追加——没有接受身份，因此无哈希可调和——拒绝整个清单，两个存储与每个 body 均原样保留，并要求显式点名 **salvage 范围**。
- `restoreExecutionBackup` 绝不回滚 body：它在替换前以精确摘要重新冻结（审批后发生变动的 body 拒绝），在持久收据上记录 `retainedDigest`/`retainedLiveDigest`/`retainedDifferences`，使 body 集合的崩溃同样可判定，并在收据上返回。epoch 推进、stale 拒绝与历史 id 保留不变；`requiredRebind()` 明确说明保留 body 不回滚。
- §4.3 maintenance exclusion 现在只在 `store-activation.ts` 中拼写一次（`withExecutionMaintenanceLock`），由恢复模块导入而非重复；`backupStoreUnderExclusion` 是已持有该锁的调用方（恢复自身的安全点）所用的同一冻结。
- 测试：新增 `execution-populated.test.ts`（populated 图在真实校验覆盖率下 staged/activated，外加恢复与拒绝周期）、`execution-recovery.test.ts` 新增 `Phase 2b` 用例、`store-activation.test.ts` 新增 `retained bodies` 用例。
