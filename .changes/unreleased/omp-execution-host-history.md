- Added the **pure OMP hidden-history reader/exporter** (`packages/omp/src/execution-history.ts`): `readExecutionHostHistory` classifies one session ledger by the five exact hidden `customType` literals (`mstar:phase2`, `mstar:phase2-continuation`, `mstar:phase2-checkpoint`, `mstar:phase2-launch-reservation`, `mstar:model-handoff`), keeps native order/entry id, preserves every payload verbatim with its canonical sha256 digest, and decodes an advisory view (schema generation, checkpoint/operation identity, dedup key, cancellation and one-shot handoff state) that stays separate from the raw evidence. `exportExecutionHostHistory` serializes the history as canonical evidence.
- Nothing here is authority: the reader takes no session argument, produces no `ExecutionBinding`, and retains a stale or foreign `coordinatorSessionPath` as provenance only. Unrelated entry types stay unrelated, and a recognized hidden entry that cannot be decoded is retained and diagnosed (`payload-not-object`, `payload-unsupported`, `payload-generation-unverified`, `payload-record-invalid`, `payload-kind-invalid`, `payload-state-invalid`, `payload-action-invalid`, `entry-id-missing`, `entry-shape`) instead of being guessed into a session or another type. Native extension wiring lands with the OMP adoption task.
- Decoding is limited to a **verified producer schema**: only `mstar:phase2` and `mstar:model-handoff` payloads that satisfy their producer's own `version: 1` restore guard (mirrored field for field, including kind/action/state sets and the required session, workflow, checkpoint and handoff fields) produce an advisory view. The three names with no producer anywhere in the repository are retained as raw evidence and diagnosed `payload-generation-unverified` — no legacy schema and no identity is ever guessed from one, and a diagnosed entry publishes no derived fact at all.
- Evidence is never dropped or weakened: a payload the canonical form cannot admit keeps its native value in full with no digest (`payloadHash: null`, never fabricated), and `exportExecutionHostHistory` refuses explicitly (`ExecutionHostHistoryExportRefusal`) instead of exporting with that evidence missing or rewritten.

- 新增**纯 OMP hidden-history 读取/导出模块**（`packages/omp/src/execution-history.ts`）：`readExecutionHostHistory` 按五个精确 hidden `customType` 字面量（`mstar:phase2`、`mstar:phase2-continuation`、`mstar:phase2-checkpoint`、`mstar:phase2-launch-reservation`、`mstar:model-handoff`）分类单个 session ledger，保留原生顺序与 entry id，逐条原样保留 payload 及其 canonical sha256 摘要，并解出与原始证据分离的 advisory view（schema generation、checkpoint/operation 身份、dedup key、取消与一次性 handoff 状态）；`exportExecutionHostHistory` 将该历史序列化为 canonical 证据。
- 该模块不构成任何权威：读取不接受 session 参数、不产出 `ExecutionBinding`，陈旧或外来的 `coordinatorSessionPath` 仅作为 provenance 保留。无关 entry 类型保持无关；无法解码的已识别 hidden entry 会被保留并诊断（`payload-not-object`、`payload-unsupported`、`payload-generation-unverified`、`payload-record-invalid`、`payload-kind-invalid`、`payload-state-invalid`、`payload-action-invalid`、`entry-id-missing`、`entry-shape`），而不会被猜成某个 session 或另一种类型。原生扩展接线随 OMP adoption 任务落地。
- 解码仅限**已核验的 producer schema**：只有满足 producer 自身 `version: 1` restore guard（逐字段镜像，含 kind/action/state 集合与必需的 session、workflow、checkpoint、handoff 字段）的 `mstar:phase2` / `mstar:model-handoff` payload 才会产生 advisory view；仓库中没有任何 producer 的三个类型只保留原始证据并诊断 `payload-generation-unverified`——绝不猜测 legacy schema 或其身份，且被诊断的 entry 不发布任何派生事实。
- 证据绝不丢失或削弱：canonical 形式无法接纳的 payload 完整保留原生值且不带摘要（`payloadHash: null`，绝不伪造），`exportExecutionHostHistory` 会显式拒绝（`ExecutionHostHistoryExportRefusal`），而不是在证据缺失或被改写的情况下导出。
