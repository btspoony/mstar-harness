- **Recovery-first engine surface.** The exported lifecycle operations (`mutatePlanCoordination`, `mutateExecutionPlan`, `mutateExecutionWorkflow`, `amendPrepareWorkflow`, the registration producers and the closes) now accept a sparse intent — omitted redundant selectors, revisions, session projections and copied metadata — and resolve it against the caller's trusted root, its durable association and the recorded declarations, while their transaction-local inputs stay strict. The resolver is published: `resolveIntentRoot`, `resolveIntentTarget` and `resolveCurrentAuthority` (the durable authority generation a DB-route caller re-asserts before committing). No convenience wrapper is published beside the resolved path.
- **One recovery sidecar on every outcome.** A successful engine value carries `recovery` and a refusal carries the same object under `error.details.recovery` (`outcome`, `target`, `applied`, `unresolved`, `resolvedFrom`, `warnings`, `commitState`), so a caller learns what landed, what stayed unresolved, the one decision that remains and how much of the call committed — a partially applied compound intent is never reported as a mutation-free refusal.
- **Semantic replay instead of transport freshness.** A repeated intent is a current success with no revision, timestamp, receipt or byte churn (`already-satisfied`, `commitState: "none"`); a revision that moved without the intent changing is a drift warning rather than a refusal; a genuinely different payload or target still refuses; and a superseded receipt or authority generation reports its own typed cause instead of replaying an old epoch. An already-satisfied effect is answered without re-reading the evidence it once read, and a proven merge is reused.
- **Registration derives its own identity.** `derivePlanRegistration` and `deriveLifecyclePhase` derive a selected plan's id/title/canonical pointer and catalog location, and an iteration's phase, with the selected document as the authority; an interrupted registration finishes its missing link on retry while preserving the first creation identity and timestamps, and a phase-less iteration is derived on read (`workflow.snapshot.derived-phase`) and persisted by the next authorized mutation.
- **A Prepare amendment commits per component.** `amendPrepareWorkflow` applies the independent components it can and reports the withheld ones, one minimum decision each, in the stable `append plan <id>` / `correct-plan-file <id>` / `integration-worktree <path>` / `execution-policy <value>` vocabulary — one partial receipt per call on both authority routes, and no applied component is ever applied twice on replay.
- **Every declared lifecycle state has a writer, on both routes.** Evidence recorded before a row is `Done` is legal and completes the report-only tail; `closeFileWorkflow` is the file authority's ONE close verb (fulfilment → row `Done` → terminal membership removal → unregister) and the DB route composes the same intent on one handle; explicit `failed`/`stopped` outcomes are recordable on both routes, settling only the claims the lifecycle owns whose holder is not live, preserving the first `ended_at`, and never rewriting a recorded outcome.
- **Additive exports only.** New: `closeFileWorkflow`, `resolveIntentRoot`, `resolveIntentTarget`, `resolveCurrentAuthority`, `deriveLifecyclePhase`, `derivePlanRegistration`, `PREPARE_PHASE` / `EXECUTE_PHASE` / `CLOSE_PHASE`, `DERIVED_PHASE_CODE`, `CloseWorkflowOutcome` and the resolution/derivation/close types (`RootResolution`, `TargetResolution`, `AuthorityVerdict`, `FileWorkflowCloseInput`, `FileWorkflowCloseResult`, …). No export was renamed or removed; internal frames, builders and test hooks stay module-scoped.

- **面向恢复的引擎接口。** 导出的生命周期操作（`mutatePlanCoordination`、`mutateExecutionPlan`、`mutateExecutionWorkflow`、`amendPrepareWorkflow`、注册生产者与各类 close）现在接受**稀疏意图**——可省略冗余选择器、修订号、会话投影与复制的元数据——并依据调用者可信根、其持久关联与已记录声明完成解析；事务内的实际输入仍然严格。解析器已发布：`resolveIntentRoot`、`resolveIntentTarget`、`resolveCurrentAuthority`（DB 路线调用者在提交前重新确认的持久权威代）。严格路径旁不发布任何便利包装。
- **每个结果都带同一份恢复副载。** 成功值携带 `recovery`，拒绝则在 `error.details.recovery` 下携带同一对象（`outcome`、`target`、`applied`、`unresolved`、`resolvedFrom`、`warnings`、`commitState`）：调用者据此得知已落地的部分、仍未解决的部分、仅剩的那个决定，以及本次调用提交到哪里——部分应用的复合意图绝不报告为「未发生变更的拒绝」。
- **以语义重放取代传输时效。** 重复的意图就是当前成功：不推进修订号、不写时间戳、不产生回执或字节抖动（`already-satisfied`、`commitState: "none"`）；意图未变而修订号变化只是漂移告警而非拒绝；载荷或目标确实不同仍会拒绝；被取代的回执或权威代报告自己的类型化原因，绝不重放旧 epoch。已满足的效应不必重读它当初读取的证据，已证实的合并会被复用。
- **注册自行派生身份。** `derivePlanRegistration` 与 `deriveLifecyclePhase` 以所选文档为权威，派生 plan 的 id/标题/规范指针与 catalog 位置、以及迭代的 phase；被中断的注册在重试时补齐缺失的那一环，并保留最初的创建身份与时间戳；缺少 phase 的迭代在读取时派生（`workflow.snapshot.derived-phase`），由下一次获授权变更持久化。
- **Prepare 修订按组件提交。** `amendPrepareWorkflow` 提交能独立落地的组件，并报告被扣留的组件（每个组件给出一个最小决定），使用稳定词汇 `append plan <id>` / `correct-plan-file <id>` / `integration-worktree <path>` / `execution-policy <value>`——两种权威路线都给出一次部分回执，且重放时已应用的组件绝不二次应用。
- **每个已声明的生命周期状态在两条路线上都有写入者。** 在行 `Done` **之前**记录证据是合法的，并可完成 report-only 尾段；`closeFileWorkflow` 是文件权威唯一的 close 动词（履行 → 行 `Done` → 终态成员移除 → 注销），DB 路线在同一个 handle 上组合同一意图；显式 `failed`/`stopped` 终态在两条路线上都可记录，仅结算本生命周期拥有且持有者已不活跃的占用、保留首个 `ended_at`，并且绝不改写已记录的终态。
- **仅做增量导出。** 新增：`closeFileWorkflow`、`resolveIntentRoot`、`resolveIntentTarget`、`resolveCurrentAuthority`、`deriveLifecyclePhase`、`derivePlanRegistration`、`PREPARE_PHASE` / `EXECUTE_PHASE` / `CLOSE_PHASE`、`DERIVED_PHASE_CODE`、`CloseWorkflowOutcome` 以及解析/派生/close 相关类型（`RootResolution`、`TargetResolution`、`AuthorityVerdict`、`FileWorkflowCloseInput`、`FileWorkflowCloseResult` 等）。没有任何导出被改名或删除；内部框架、构造器与测试钩子仍局限于模块内。
