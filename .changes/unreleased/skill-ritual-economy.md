- **Engine-check callouts collapsed to one contract.** The 46 `**Engine check (when available):**` blockquotes across `skills/**` are now one-line callouts that name their exact command, plus one shared contract in `mstar-use-cli/references/checks-and-lints.md` § Engine check callouts. `scripts/skill-callout-contract.ts` now owns the marker and the pointer form; `drift-lint.ts` and `standalone-smoke.ts` both consume it, so Guard 1 (CLI-citation and engine-import validation) and Guard 6 (duplicate-body detection) cover **48** callouts instead of 46 — two pre-existing `**Engine check:**` blockquotes were previously invisible to both guards — and the standalone check asserts a non-zero callout count plus the shared-contract pointer on every one, so a renamed or broken scan can no longer pass vacuously. `CONCEPTS.md` defines the guarded form.
- **PM dispatch ceremony replaced by conditions.** `mstar-roles/references/project-manager.md` drops the printable 14-question self-check and the 16-field `Pre-implement Gate Check` receipt; the surviving conditions are one `## Dispatch preconditions (hard block)` list, and the Task Board section keeps only what the plan rows do not carry. `mstar-dispatch-gates` collapses its two leaf-executor anti-recursion sections into one block that points at the existing owner in `mstar-roles/references/_shared/leaf-executor-core.md`. `.cursor/skills/mstar-routing-eval/assets/routing-evals.json` retargets four assertions that quoted the deleted receipt fields, keeping their `hard_fail_if` triggers firing.
- **Evidence selection and test reliability gained a home.** `mstar-coding-behavior/references/evidence-selection.md` maps a change type to the smallest sufficient evidence; `references/test-reliability.md` owns execution topology, atomic resource allocation, process-global containment, timeout budgets, disposal to quiescence, regression proof and the rejection of flake-masking — pointing at `.mstar/knowledge/testing-patterns/verification-redundancy-and-unholdable-gates.md` for the subjects it already owns instead of restating them.
- **Context economy, prose truth, and a deletion criterion.** `mstar-skill-authoring/references/context-economy.md` owns what model-facing text costs per turn (minimal start, explicit discovery, visible constraints, bounded outputs, locality, total-work evaluation, plus three tool-definition rules and the before/after measurement rule). `mstar-prose` gains a fact-check rule (a claim about an operation, default or error is admissible only if it was run against the current tree; it judges truth, the HEAD test judges resolvability). `mstar-knowledge` Maintain gains the future-decision-value criterion (delete / keep / archive / never archive a live proposal / keep a rejection while it stays tempting), consolidating three fragments that already lived there.
- **Simplify audit proof bar.** `mstar-audit/references/audit-playbook.md` § 5 states the six-clause proof bar for a removal candidate (current owner, producer-to-consumer path, what disappears, what remains, the capability or compatibility given up, the strongest retain reason) and grades each rejection as unreachable-code removal, narrower public behaviour with the loss stated, or a protected obligation.

- **PM 派发仪式改为条件清单。** `mstar-roles/references/project-manager.md` 删除可打印的 14 问自检与 16 字段 `Pre-implement Gate Check` 回执，保留的条件合并为一份 `## Dispatch preconditions (hard block)`；Task Board 一节只保留 plan 行没有承载的内容。`mstar-dispatch-gates` 的两节承接方反递归文本折叠为一段，指向 `mstar-roles/references/_shared/leaf-executor-core.md` 中既有的所有者。`.cursor/skills/mstar-routing-eval/assets/routing-evals.json` 重定向四条引用了已删除回执字段的断言，其 `hard_fail_if` 触发条件仍然生效。
