- Cut the canonical CLI/session/host corpus over to the **ACTIVE execution transport** of the phase-2b contract §3.2: every active example and child handoff now writes under an **independently acquired identity** with `--session-ref` + the addressed scope's **full execution token** as `--expect` + the caller's `--operation` id, and reads/tokens come from `mstar status validate` or the scope read rather than from a remembered revision (`mstar-use-cli` ×4, `mstar-artifacts` ×2, `mstar-iteration` ×3, `mstar-roles` ×2, `commands/iteration-drive.md`, `mstar-host/SKILL.md`).

- Made the **reference a lookup, not a bearer credential**: it names a stored session row and grants nothing without the caller the engine compares inside its own transaction; a reference, a `--expect` value (revision or full token) and an operation id are never forwarded into a child Assignment or invocation, and the scoped route's finish/done boundary is unchanged (**resume ≠ recovery**: a stopped owner is replaced only by `mstar session recover` on the active authority, or by the guarded Prepare recovery on the file route).
